## Sources
The material in this section is based on the following sources, which are provided for your reference and transparency. 
The sources have served as inspiration for the content in this section and modified for this project as needed. 
Please note that the content of these sources may be out of date.
The references listed below also serve as additional resources for to be explored if you want to go deeper into the topic.


### Books:
- E. R. Tufte, The Visual Display of Quantitative Information, 2nd edition, Graphics Press (2001)
- C. O. Wilke, Fundamentals of Data Visualization, O'Reilly (2019), free online: https://clauswilke.com/dataviz/
- C. Nussbaumer Knaflic, Storytelling with Data, Wiley (2015)
- T. Munzner, Visualization Analysis and Design, CRC Press (2014)

### Tutorials:

- Plotly - range slider and selector: https://plotly.com/python/range-slider/
- Plotly - subplots: https://plotly.com/python/subplots/
- Plotly - templates and theming: https://plotly.com/python/templates/
- Plotly - interactive HTML export: https://plotly.com/python/interactive-html-export/
- Plotly - static image export: https://plotly.com/python/static-image-export/
