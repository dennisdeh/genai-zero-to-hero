## Sources
The material in this section is based on the following sources, which are provided for your reference and transparency. 
The sources have served as inspiration for the content in this section and modified for this project as needed. 
Please note that the content of these sources may be out of date.
The references listed below also serve as additional resources for to be explored if you want to go deeper into the topic.


### Books:
- Sebastian Raschka, Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch) (Manning, 2024)
- Jay Alammar and Maarten Grootendorst, Hands-On Large Language Models (O'Reilly, 2024)

### Tutorials:
- PyTorch Tutorials: https://docs.pytorch.org/tutorials/
- Jay Alammar, The Illustrated Transformer: https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/
- Hugging Face LLM Course, chapter on tokenizers (BPE): https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/chapter6/1
- Hugging Face tokenizers documentation: https://huggingface.co/docs/tokenizers/index

### Articles:
- Vaswani et al. (2017), Attention Is All You Need: https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762
- Radford et al. (2018), Improving Language Understanding by Generative Pre-Training (GPT-1): https://cdn.openai.com/research-covers/language-unsupervised/language_understanding_paper.pdf
- Radford et al. (2019), Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners (GPT-2): https://cdn.openai.com/better-language-models/language_models_are_unsupervised_multitask_learners.pdf
- Brown et al. (2020), Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (GPT-3): https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165
- Sennrich et al. (2016), Neural Machine Translation of Rare Words with Subword Units (BPE): https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.07909
- Ba et al. (2016), Layer Normalization: https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.06450
- Hendrycks and Gimpel (2016), Gaussian Error Linear Units (GELUs): https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08415
- Press and Wolf (2017), Using the Output Embedding to Improve Language Models (weight tying): https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05859
- Holtzman et al. (2019), The Curious Case of Neural Text Degeneration (nucleus sampling): https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.09751
- Kaplan et al. (2020), Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models: https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361
- Hoffmann et al. (2022), Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models (Chinchilla): https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556
