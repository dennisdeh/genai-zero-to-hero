## Sources
The material in this section is based on the following sources, which are provided for your reference and transparency. 
The sources have served as inspiration for the content in this section and modified for this project as needed. 
Please note that the content of these sources may be out of date.
The references listed below also serve as additional resources for to be explored if you want to go deeper into the topic.


### Books:
- Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville – Deep Learning, MIT Press, 2016 (chapter 9: convolutional networks): https://www.deeplearningbook.org
- Zhang, Lipton, Li, Smola – Dive into Deep Learning (convolutional neural networks, modern CNNs): https://d2l.ai

### Tutorials:
- Stanford CS231n course notes – Convolutional Neural Networks: https://cs231n.github.io/convolutional-networks/
- PyTorch tutorial – Training a classifier (CIFAR-10): https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/blitz/cifar10_tutorial.html
- PyTorch tutorial – Saving and loading models: https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/saving_loading_models.html
- torchvision documentation (datasets, transforms v2): https://pytorch.org/vision/stable/index.html
- scikit-learn – load_digits: https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.datasets.load_digits.html
- Dumoulin, Visin – A guide to convolution arithmetic for deep learning, 2016: https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.07285

### Articles:
- LeCun, Bottou, Bengio, Haffner – Gradient-based learning applied to document recognition (LeNet), Proceedings of the IEEE, 1998
- Krizhevsky, Sutskever, Hinton – ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks (AlexNet), NeurIPS 2012
- Simonyan, Zisserman – Very Deep Convolutional Networks for Large-Scale Image Recognition (VGG), 2014: https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.1556
- Ioffe, Szegedy – Batch Normalization: Accelerating Deep Network Training by Reducing Internal Covariate Shift, 2015: https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03167
- He, Zhang, Ren, Sun – Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition (ResNet), 2015: https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385
- Zeiler, Fergus – Visualizing and Understanding Convolutional Networks, 2013: https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.2901
- Simonyan, Vedaldi, Zisserman – Deep Inside Convolutional Networks: Visualising Image Classification Models and Saliency Maps, 2013: https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.6034
- Szegedy et al. – Rethinking the Inception Architecture for Computer Vision (label smoothing), 2015: https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00567
- Xiao, Rasul, Vollgraf – Fashion-MNIST: a Novel Image Dataset for Benchmarking Machine Learning Algorithms, 2017: https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.07747

### Videos lectures:
- Stanford CS231n – Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition (course website with lecture materials): https://cs231n.stanford.edu

### Code Repositories:
- pytorch/vision (torchvision models and datasets): https://github.com/pytorch/vision
- zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist: https://github.com/zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist
