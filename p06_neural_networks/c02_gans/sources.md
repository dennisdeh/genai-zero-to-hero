## Sources
The material in this section is based on the following sources, which are provided for your reference and transparency. 
The sources have served as inspiration for the content in this section and modified for this project as needed. 
Please note that the content of these sources may be out of date.
The references listed below also serve as additional resources for to be explored if you want to go deeper into the topic.


### Books:
- Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville – Deep Learning, MIT Press, 2016 (chapter 20: deep generative models): https://www.deeplearningbook.org
- Zhang, Lipton, Li, Smola – Dive into Deep Learning (generative adversarial networks, DCGAN): https://d2l.ai

### Tutorials:
- PyTorch tutorial – DCGAN: https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/dcgan_faces_tutorial.html
- Goodfellow – NIPS 2016 Tutorial: Generative Adversarial Networks, 2016: https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.00160

### Articles:
- Goodfellow, Pouget-Abadie, Mirza, Xu, Warde-Farley, Ozair, Courville, Bengio – Generative Adversarial Networks, 2014: https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2661
- Radford, Metz, Chintala – Unsupervised Representation Learning with Deep Convolutional Generative Adversarial Networks (DCGAN), 2015: https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06434
- Salimans, Goodfellow, Zaremba, Cheung, Radford, Chen – Improved Techniques for Training GANs, 2016: https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.03498
- Metz, Poole, Pfau, Sohl-Dickstein – Unrolled Generative Adversarial Networks (mixture-of-Gaussians mode-collapse experiments), 2016: https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.02163
- Arjovsky, Chintala, Bottou – Wasserstein GAN, 2017: https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.07875
- Gulrajani, Ahmed, Arjovsky, Dumoulin, Courville – Improved Training of Wasserstein GANs (WGAN-GP), 2017: https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.00028
- Heusel, Ramsauer, Unterthiner, Nessler, Hochreiter – GANs Trained by a Two Time-Scale Update Rule Converge to a Local Nash Equilibrium (FID), 2017: https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.08500
- Karras, Laine, Aila – A Style-Based Generator Architecture for Generative Adversarial Networks (StyleGAN), 2018: https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.04948
