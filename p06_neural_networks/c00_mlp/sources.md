## Sources
The material in this section is based on the following sources, which are provided for your reference and transparency. 
The sources have served as inspiration for the content in this section and modified for this project as needed. 
Please note that the content of these sources may be out of date.
The references listed below also serve as additional resources for to be explored if you want to go deeper into the topic.


### Books:
- Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville – Deep Learning, MIT Press, 2016 (chapters 6–8: deep feed-forward networks, regularisation, optimisation): https://www.deeplearningbook.org
- Michael Nielsen – Neural Networks and Deep Learning (online book, backpropagation explained step by step): http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com
- Zhang, Lipton, Li, Smola – Dive into Deep Learning (multilayer perceptrons, optimisation algorithms): https://d2l.ai

### Tutorials:
- PyTorch – Learn the Basics (tensors, datasets and data loaders, building models, autograd, optimisation): https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/basics/intro.html
- PyTorch documentation – torch.nn, torch.optim, torch.nn.init: https://pytorch.org/docs/stable/index.html
- Stanford CS231n course notes – Backpropagation, intuitions: https://cs231n.github.io/optimization-2/
- Stanford CS231n course notes – Neural networks (setting up the architecture, data and loss, learning): https://cs231n.github.io/neural-networks-1/

### Articles:
- Rumelhart, Hinton, Williams – Learning representations by back-propagating errors, Nature 323, 1986
- Cybenko – Approximation by superpositions of a sigmoidal function, Mathematics of Control, Signals and Systems 2, 1989
- Hornik – Approximation capabilities of multilayer feedforward networks, Neural Networks 4, 1991
- Glorot, Bengio – Understanding the difficulty of training deep feedforward neural networks, AISTATS 2010
- He, Zhang, Ren, Sun – Delving Deep into Rectifiers (He initialisation), 2015: https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.01852
- Srivastava, Hinton, Krizhevsky, Sutskever, Salakhutdinov – Dropout: A Simple Way to Prevent Neural Networks from Overfitting, JMLR 15, 2014
- Kingma, Ba – Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization, 2014: https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6980
- Loshchilov, Hutter – Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization (AdamW), 2017: https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05101

### Videos lectures:
- 3Blue1Brown – Neural networks (series on gradient descent and backpropagation): https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDNU6R1_67000Dx_ZCJB-3pi
- Andrej Karpathy – The spelled-out intro to neural networks and backpropagation: building micrograd: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VMj-3S1tku0

### Code Repositories:
- karpathy/micrograd – a tiny scalar-valued autograd engine: https://github.com/karpathy/micrograd
- pytorch/examples: https://github.com/pytorch/examples
