## Sources
The material in this section is based on the following sources, which are provided for your reference and transparency. 
The sources have served as inspiration for the content in this section and modified for this project as needed. 
Please note that the content of these sources may be out of date.
The references listed below also serve as additional resources for to be explored if you want to go deeper into the topic.


### Books:
- William L. Hamilton – Graph Representation Learning, Morgan & Claypool, 2020: https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~wlh/grl_book/
- Bronstein, Bruna, Cohen, Veličković – Geometric Deep Learning: Grids, Groups, Graphs, Geodesics, and Gauges, 2021: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.13478

### Tutorials:
- PyTorch Geometric documentation: https://pytorch-geometric.readthedocs.io
- PyTorch Geometric – Creating Message Passing Networks: https://pytorch-geometric.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorial/create_gnn.html
- PyTorch Geometric – Colab notebooks and video tutorials: https://pytorch-geometric.readthedocs.io/en/latest/get_started/colabs.html
- Sanchez-Lengeling, Reif, Pearce, Wiltschko – A Gentle Introduction to Graph Neural Networks, Distill, 2021: https://distill.pub/2021/gnn-intro/

### Articles:
- Kipf, Welling – Semi-Supervised Classification with Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN), 2016: https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.02907
- Veličković, Cucurull, Casanova, Romero, Liò, Bengio – Graph Attention Networks (GAT), 2017: https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10903
- Hamilton, Ying, Leskovec – Inductive Representation Learning on Large Graphs (GraphSAGE), 2017: https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.02216
- Xu, Hu, Leskovec, Jegelka – How Powerful are Graph Neural Networks? (GIN), 2018: https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00826
- Gilmer, Schoenholz, Riley, Vinyals, Dahl – Neural Message Passing for Quantum Chemistry, 2017: https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.01212
- Fey, Lenssen – Fast Graph Representation Learning with PyTorch Geometric, 2019: https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.02428
- Yang, Cohen, Salakhutdinov – Revisiting Semi-Supervised Learning with Graph Embeddings (Planetoid splits of Cora), 2016: https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.08861
- Morris, Kriege, Bause, Kersting, Mutzel, Neumann – TUDataset: A collection of benchmark datasets for learning with graphs, 2020: https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.08663
- Zachary – An Information Flow Model for Conflict and Fission in Small Groups, Journal of Anthropological Research 33, 1977
